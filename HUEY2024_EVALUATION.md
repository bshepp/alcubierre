## 1. What the paper constructs

Planar-symmetric "chimeric" spacetimes in $1+(N+1)$ dimensions ($N=2$ parallel directions for 3+1), signature $(+,-,-,-)$: bulk regions glued along **codimension-1 membranes** (branes) carrying perfect-fluid surface stress $S^\mu{}_\nu=(\rho+p)u^\mu u_\nu-(u\!\cdot\!u)\,p\,h^\mu{}_\nu$. The Israel jump of extrinsic curvature across each brane appears as the brane's **acceleration** in each adjoining bulk; the paper tunes bulk parameters and brane equations of state so the whole stack accelerates in the exterior.

Bulks used: exterior **Minkowski** (A); two **Λ–K** regions (B, D) — a planar analogue of Schwarzschild–de Sitter with vacuum energy $\rho_\Lambda$ and an integration constant $K$ playing the role of a mass; an interior **Modified-Minkowski** region (C) — Minkowski with a static perfect-fluid boundary layer ($\kappa\rho>\kappa p>0$) whose purpose is to make the energy density on the inner branes **positive** (the paper states that a direct Minkowski|Λ–K junction on the side it needs "requires negative energy density on that brane"). Four branes in all. The sub-luminal model has all branes timelike; the super-luminal model makes the exterior branes' world-paths spacelike ($\xi\eta=-1$), with the brane fluid "generalised" accordingly.

Claims: WEC, NEC and DEC hold on every brane and in every bulk given $\rho\ge0$ and equations of state in $[-1,1]$; SEC is violated by the Λ bulks; "super-luminal warp-drive is possible that satisfies the WEC." Stated caveats: toy models; **branes are non-compact** (infinite planar extent); no stability analysis; no quantum effects; no assembly from Minkowski initial data; realistic models "would need the branes to be closed."

## 3. Placement against the project

1. **It is not a warp drive in the project's sense.** There is no shift vector carrying an interior; region C is static Minkowski bounded by branes that are themselves accelerating through the Λ–K bulks. The "passenger" is at rest in C; what moves is the stack relative to the exterior Minkowski frame, and that relative motion is only meaningful through the junctions — with planar symmetry the two Minkowski regions share no asymptotic frame.
2. **Planar symmetry is the loophole, and it is the known one.** The configuration is **not asymptotically flat** (infinite planar branes), so the Path-2A ADM 4-momentum obstruction (Task 2A.10: no in-vacuum self-acceleration of an asymptotically flat, DEC-respecting, ejecta-free body) simply does not apply — the same exit Barzegar et al. 2026 name in their discussion (drop asymptotic flatness). In the project's own language this is the **k = 0 corner of the codimension-counting law** ([`speculation/CODIMENSION_SCALING.md`](speculation/CODIMENSION_SCALING.md), Session 16; slab datum [`slab_patch.ipynb`](slab_patch.ipynb)): the planar case is exactly where the linear-in-$\beta$ obstruction vanishes and only the quadratic volumetric term survives, so a WEC-clean planar junction is *expected* and says nothing about closed bubbles.
3. **Closing the branes re-imports everything.** A compact brane stack is asymptotically flat, so ADM/Bondi balance returns: the stack can then accelerate only by emitting (Le 2026b's photon-rocket shell is precisely the compact, exact, surface-DEC version of "accelerating cavity bounded by a thin shell", and it costs $m_f/m_i=e^{-3d}$). The author's own caveat that realistic models "would need the branes to be closed" is where the project's results apply, negatively.
4. **Super-luminal branch.** A spacelike brane world-path with a "generalised" perfect fluid ($u\!\cdot\!u=-1$) is outside any matter model the project admits (Le 2026c's material-support question applies a fortiori); the super-luminal claim is a statement about a distributional stress on a spacelike hypersurface, not about a drive. Not pursued.

## 4. Disposition

Phase-4 reopening trigger (a) **evaluated — not triggered.** The construction is EC-clean where it says it is (we confirmed the three structural ingredients), but it achieves this by (i) planar non-compact geometry that evades asymptotic flatness, (ii) a static interior with no shift, and (iii) for the super-luminal case a spacelike "fluid". Within the project's slice definitions it is not a warp drive and does not contradict 2A.10, SSV 2021, or any certified result. Recorded in [`LITERATURE.md`](LITERATURE.md) §12 and NOGO_CONFRONTATION §4.5 (as the planar exit from asymptotic flatness).

**Reopening criteria for this item:** (a) a compact (closed-brane) version with explicit surface-DEC bookkeeping *and* an interior shift; (b) an asymptotically flat completion of the Λ–K stack that preserves brane WEC.
