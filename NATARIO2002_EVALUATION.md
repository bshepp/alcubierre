## 1. What the paper contains

**Section 1 (the class).** Warp drive spacetime = globally hyperbolic, flat Cauchy slices, unit lapse, bounded smooth shift $\vec X$. Results: Eulerian observers ($n = \partial_t + \vec X$) are free-fall (Prop 1.3); $K_{ij} = \frac{1}{2}(\partial_i X_j + \partial_j X_i)$, the rate-of-strain tensor of fluid mechanics (Prop 1.4); the volume expansion is $\theta = \nabla\cdot\vec X$ (Cor 1.5); the spacetime is flat wherever $\vec X$ is a Euclidean Killing field (Cor 1.6). **Theorem 1.7:** a non-flat warp drive violates WEC or SEC — SEC + Raychaudhuri force $\theta \equiv 0$, and then the Hamiltonian constraint gives $\rho_E = \frac{1}{16\pi}(\theta^2 - K_{ij}K^{ij}) = -\frac{1}{16\pi}K_{ij}K^{ij} \le 0$, with equality iff $K \equiv 0$ (flat). Example 1.8 instantiates Alcubierre 1994 ($\theta = v f' \frac{x - x_s}{r_s}$, $\rho = -\frac{v^2}{32\pi}f'^2\frac{y^2+z^2}{r_s^2}$).

**Section 2 (the construction).** In spherical coordinates about the motion axis, $\vec X \sim -v(t)\, d\!\left[f(r)\, r^2 \sin^2\theta\, d\varphi\right]$ — the vector dual of an exact 2-form, hence **divergence-free by construction**: $X^{\hat r} = -2vf\cos\theta$, $X^{\hat\theta} = v(2f + rf')\sin\theta$, with $f = 0$ near the origin and $f = \frac{1}{2}$ at large $r$ (comoving convention: flat interior at rest, exterior sliding at $-v\,\partial_x$). Six explicit $K_{ij}$ components; $\theta = K_{rr} + K_{\theta\theta} + K_{\varphi\varphi} = 0$ exactly; the radial compression in front is exactly balanced by perpendicular expansion. The energy density is the $\rho$ formula quoted above — strictly negative on the wall.

**Section 3 (superluminal optics — NOT reproduced; outside the subluminal slice).** For $v_s > 1$: stationarity is impossible ($\langle\partial_t,\partial_t\rangle = -1 + \|X\|^2$); a Mach-cone-like horizon at $\sin\alpha = 1/\|X\|$; a visibility horizon; the thin-wall refraction law $\sin r = \sin i / (1 + v_s\cos(i-\theta))$; the energy relation $E(1 + \vec X\cdot\vec n) = E_0$ with **infinite blueshift at the horizon**. Noted: this blueshift result is a geometric-optics precursor of the Finazzi–Liberati–Barceló 2009 semiclassical front-horizon instability (RSET divergence) — relevant to the fence programme's ranked crack #1, not to this repo's classical slices. The claimed thin-wall limit of Clark–Hiscock–Larson 1999 was also not checked.

## 3. Sharpenings beyond the paper

1. **No marginal directions.** Alcubierre's violation density $\propto f'^2\sin^2\theta$ vanishes on the motion axis; Natário's $\rho \propto -[3f'^2\cos^2\theta + (f'+\frac{r}{2}f'')^2\sin^2\theta]$ is strictly negative at every wall point *including* the axis (battery: wall WEC-violation fraction exactly 1.0, axis strict). Killing the expansion makes the pointwise violation *ubiquitous*, not milder.
2. **Hawking–Ellis Type IV dominance, worsening at small $v$.** The paper checks only $\rho_E$. The full Eulerian-frame $T_{\hat\mu\hat\nu}$ has energy flux $\propto v$ against density/stress $\propto v^2$, so the flux dominates as $v \to 0$: at $v = 0.1$, **98.4%** of wall points have complex stress-energy eigenvalues (Type IV — no rest frame, no principal-pressure decomposition, all pointwise ECs violated); at $v = 1$, 77.4%. The subluminal limit is *more* algebraically pathological, not less — even though $\rho_E \to 0$ quadratically. (Alcubierre shows the same phenomenon: 85.7% Type IV at $v=1$; Rodal 2025's "Type IV pockets present" row is confirmed for both.)
3. **The wall ergo-band.** $\|X\|$ is not monotone from interior 0 to exterior $v$: the $(2f + rf')$ term overshoots, $\sup\|X\| \approx (R_0\sigma/4 + O(1))\,v = 5.53\,v$ at $(R_0,\sigma) = (5,4)$. So $\partial_t$ goes spacelike inside the wall (an ergo-band; the paper's Section-3 surface $\|X\| = 1$) already at $v^* = 0.181$ — far below the asymptotic threshold $v = 1$ the paper works with, and $v^*$ *falls* as the wall thins ($\sim 4/R_0\sigma$). A thin-wall zero-expansion drive is locally trans-critical at modest cruise speeds.
4. **Independent confirmation of Rodal 2025's comparison table.** Our exact-symbolic pipeline reproduces his plot-derived Natário/Alcubierre ratios within 3–10% (64.9 vs ≈67; NEC 65.7 vs ≈60) at his matched parameters. This two-sidedly (a) anchors our machinery against an external computation and (b) upgrades confidence in the RODAL2025_EVALUATION observation that the headline "2,600× improvement vs Natário" is mostly a statement that *Natário is unusually bad* — the transverse-pressure/$rf''$ term carries an extra $(R_0\sigma)^2$-class factor.
5. **The repo's Slice-1 closure is Theorem 1.7 instantiated.** The Session-36 identity 2 ($\nabla\cdot\beta \equiv 0 \Rightarrow \rho_E = -K_{ij}K^{ij}/16\pi \le 0$, any profile) is exactly the WEC branch of the paper's Theorem 1.7 restricted to the solenoidal family — Natário had the general statement in 2002.
