## What this project is

A personal landscape exploration of the Alcubierre warp drive in general relativity, asking whether the standard "exotic-matter requirement" can be reformulated as a *boundary effect* rather than a substance to be manufactured. **Project mode is "surfing", not "paper-writing"** — there are no concrete deliverables; the goal is to understand the structure of the obstructions to a working classical warp drive.

After 55 sessions, the project has produced (i) a *static-slice* classical no-go for useful warp drives, (ii) a six-slice exploration of which assumptions in the no-go are load-bearing, (iii) a documented set of published candidate constructions outside the slice — three adversarially adjudicated (Fell–Heisenberg, Lentz, Garattini–Zatrimaylov), (iv) certified minimal-mass floors for the Fuchs shell family, and (v) the full disposition of the Phase-2E relaxations (time-dependence, topology, modified gravity).
