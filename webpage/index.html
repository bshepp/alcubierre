The Landscape
Four answers to "is the warp drive physical?"

Existence — Yes (Static Slice)

Fuchs et al. 2024 (arXiv:2405.02709) show a constant-velocity Alcubierre shift can be embedded in a Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff matter shell that satisfies all four energy conditions (NEC, WEC, DEC, SEC) in the bubble interior and on the wall.

Anchor point: R₁=10 m, R₂=20 m, β=0.02 c, M = R₂c²/(6G). Reproduced independently in Warp Factory v1.0 at all four ECs = 1.0000.

Thickness — Bounded From Below

The shell cannot be arbitrarily thin. Across a 27-cell (C, R₂, β) sweep at 6 inner Δ values each, the smallest DEC-respecting thickness obeys

Δmin/R2 = κ · β / C

with κ ≈ 5 typical (median 6, mean 5.3, std 1.0; range [4.0, 6.0] for 15 bracketed cells of 27).

Form universal across the surface; numerical κ is a slice-value, not a constant. Decision-gate-B per Session 19.

Acceleration — No Useful Mechanism

ADM 4-momentum conservation forbids self-acceleration of the shell in vacuum (Schuster–Santiago–Visser 2023 Theorem 3). Three escape routes catalogued; only ordinary mass ejection is viable.

Quantitative ceiling under the most favourable Fuchs-compatible parameters (HF-Jobs sweep + Varma 2022 NR rescaling): GW recoil ≲ 0.25% of vwarp. Not enough.

Static Infrastructure — No Wall

Krasnikov-tube networks: symbolic Einstein tensor reproduces Everett–Roman 1997 Eq. 14 to 14 decimals; universal scaling ρpmin ∝ −η/ε². WEC pass-rate 0.0000 across 300 (η, ε, n) points.

Closes the static-infrastructure-network branch independently of QFT loopholes.

The Slice Composite

Within the canonical static slice — Alcubierre βxx̂ shift × spherical Fuchs-class shell × asymptotically flat vacuum exterior × steady-state metric or its Lorentz boost × 4D General Relativity — the answer is "a positive-energy bubble exists, but no classical mechanism can accelerate it to useful speeds and no static infrastructure walls can be built."

This is not the closure of the classical question. Six adjacent slices (alternate shifts, hybrid walls, time-dependent acceleration, Krasnikov-2003 QI loopholes, cosmological exterior, modified gravity) are mapped in Phase 2C and all return null; the unenumerated landscape is logged with reopening criteria as Phase 2E. The three published positive-energy candidate constructions have all now been adjudicated in-pipeline (Sessions 42–46): Fell–Heisenberg's strict-pass is a box artifact, Lentz fails the full energy conditions at class level, and Garattini–Zatrimaylov reproduces exactly but is a comoving vacuum void with zero transport content.

The audit programme (Sessions 35–41)

A four-thread pre-Phase-2E deep audit re-examined every closure for process-vs-physics basis, then kill-tested all six weakened negatives. Score: four negatives strengthened (one by 72×), one confirmed and re-based, and one verdict REVERSED — the certified radial ladder shows small nested-shell mass splits improve the NEC margin ~8×, refuting the Session-26 "single shell locally optimal" record and opening a mass-reduction candidate lead.

Along the way the audit caught and fixed a frame-projection bug in the Slice-1 evaluator (its old "0.94 free-form ridge" never existed; the corrected negative is now an analytic identity) and re-based the κ scaling constant on certified crossings: κ = 4.93 ± 0.44.

Quantitative residue (Session 32): the canonical Fuchs mass is over-provisioned 1.75× — certified floor Mmin = 2.568 × 10²⁷ kg.

Three published positive-energy candidates, adjudicated (Sessions 42–46)

Fell–Heisenberg 2021: the celebrated strict-pass manifold is an evaluation-box artifact — closed-form far-field analysis shows every tested configuration violates WEC+DEC beyond a finite radius outside the box.

Lentz 2020: closed negative at class level — every compact ℓ¹ soliton strictly violates the full WEC and DEC on its outermost wavefront, at every amplitude; the paper's Eulerian positivity is exactly marginal there (and not reproducible from the published record).

Garattini–Zatrimaylov 2025: reproduced exactly — the first external construction to survive reproduction — but the Hubble-matched de Sitter bubble is exactly comoving (zero transport content) and violates ANEC on every wall-crossing null geodesic tested. Confirmed, and not a useful-warp loophole.

Where this leaves the landscape

Within 4D semiclassical GR the project currently has no known candidate for a vacuum + DEC + dynamical warp realisation. The Casimir route (Phase 2B) is closed as a physical mechanism (63.5–69.6 orders of magnitude short even granting a perfect graviton mirror); the three published positive-energy constructions are adjudicated above. Remaining open directions: the deliberately-deferred Phase-2E relaxations (first axis — the anisotropic-Π exponent — already probed and closed negative) and the nested-shell minimal-mass candidate from the audit's one reversal.
